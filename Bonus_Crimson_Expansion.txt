[THE DEEP CRIMSON PROTOCOL]

The Fractured Whispers Expansion: Focuses on heightened psychological dread and sentient reality collapse.

* Prompt B-01: Nightmare metropolis suspended upside down above endless abyss while faceless crowds walk across vertical streets.
* Prompt B-02: The sensation of your own reflection in a mirror slowly detaching itself, gaining sentience, and whispering secrets only you can hear.
* Prompt B-03: A recurring dream where familiar faces are subtly distorted, their words nonsensical, and the world around you unravels into pure chaos.
* Prompt B-04: The chilling realization that your memories are not your own, but fragments of countless other lives, merging into a fractured identity.
* Prompt B-05: Endless classroom maze where shadow children sit motionless at desks while black ink floods ceilings.
* Prompt B-06: The world outside your window slowly dissolving into static, replaced by a void that hums with an ancient, malevolent intelligence.
* Prompt B-07: A constant, low-frequency hum that only you can hear, slowly driving you to the brink of madness as it reveals hidden patterns in reality.
* Prompt B-08: Nursery mirror reflecting monstrous alternate family standing behind viewer, emotionally devastating symbolism.
* Prompt B-09: The unsettling feeling of being watched by something that exists just beyond the veil of perception, its presence a cold, creeping dread.
* Prompt B-10: Child’s drawing on bedroom wall becoming physically alive beneath flickering moonlight.
* Prompt B-11: A recurring hallucination of a monstrous entity that mirrors your deepest fears, growing more vivid and real with each appearance.
* Prompt B-12: The sudden, terrifying understanding that the people around you are not real, but elaborate constructs designed to maintain a fragile illusion.
* Prompt B-13: A hidden room in your house that appears and disappears at will, revealing glimpses of a reality that should not exist.
* Prompt B-14: The feeling of your own body slowly becoming alien to you, your limbs moving independently, your voice speaking words you didn't intend.
* Prompt B-15: A book whose words change every time you read them, slowly twisting familiar narratives into horrifying prophecies.
* Prompt B-16: The sky above you cracking like glass, revealing glimpses of an impossible, cosmic horror lurking just beyond.
* Prompt B-17: A recurring nightmare where you are trapped in an endless loop of a mundane task, each repetition subtly more terrifying than the last.
* Prompt B-18: The chilling realization that you are a character in a story, and the author is a malevolent entity toying with your existence.
* Prompt B-19: A whisper in your ear that tells you the world ended long ago, and everything you perceive is merely an echo of what once was.
* Prompt B-20: The sudden, overwhelming urge to destroy everything you hold dear, a primal instinct overriding your will, hinting at a deeper, darker influence.
